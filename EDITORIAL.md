# Editorial checklist

- Confirm each character's name, gender, family relations, affiliation, and chronology against the creator's latest canon notes before publishing.
- Treat voice actor names in `staff/index.html` as candidates until explicitly finalized.
- Treat music creators in `music/index.html` and `assets/data/music.json` as proposals, not final credits.
- Do not publish invented episode titles, plot summaries, air dates, staff appointments, news, audio, or images.
- Keep this animation project distinct from its shared-world companion work.
- Preserve the ivory / antique-gold / deep-rose palette and relative-link strategy.
